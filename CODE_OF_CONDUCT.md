# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and publication of real message data are unacceptable. Focus review on the work, assume good faith, and make room for different experience levels.

Do not report sensitive conduct concerns in a public issue. A named private contact and enforcement
procedure must be added before community contributions open; this unresolved contact is a
publication blocker recorded in `PUBLIC_GITHUB_AUDIT.md`.
