# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and publication of real message data are unacceptable. Focus review on the work, assume good faith, and make room for different experience levels.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct concerns in a public issue. Use [GitHub's platform-level Report Abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) or the applicable GitHub content-reporting control.

Provide a concise description, the relevant date and time, links to any public project activity, and the desired resolution. Do not include private mailbox data or unrelated personal information.

Reports received by the project will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information may be disclosed when required for safety, law, or enforcement. Reports sent through GitHub are also subject to GitHub's policies and processes.

Use the same GitHub reporting route if a concern involves the sole maintainer. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review the available information and consider the severity, context, and history of the conduct. Possible actions include:

- private clarification or guidance;
- a warning;
- removal or editing of project-hosted content;
- a temporary participation restriction; or
- a permanent project ban.

Enforcement decisions will be limited to project spaces and resources the maintainer controls. Platform-level reports and enforcement remain subject to GitHub's own policies and processes.
