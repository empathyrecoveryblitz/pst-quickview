# Public screenshot procedure

Use only purpose-built synthetic PST, EML, and MSG data with fictional people, `example.com` addresses, neutral subjects, and non-sensitive attachments. Never reuse real mail, diagnostics, workspace paths, Finder sidebars, account names, volume names, or notification content.

Capture light and dark modes, both layouts, folder/message/conversation states, standalone preview, and an empty state. Review every pixel at full resolution, strip metadata, and have a second person confirm that no private content appears before committing under a public screenshots directory. Existing private/local screenshots must remain ignored and uncommitted.
